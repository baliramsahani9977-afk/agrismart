01 / CROP PLANNING

Crop suitability screening

RULE BASED

A transparent screening compares mean crop-growth temperature, annual rainfall and soil pH with FAO EcoCrop ranges. It is not a trained AI model or a local agronomy recommendation. NPK, humidity, soil type and location are collected but not scored because comparable local thresholds are unavailable.

Enter mean crop-growth temperature, annual rainfall and soil pH to see a preliminary range comparison. Optional inputs are validated but are not scored.

02 / PLANT HEALTH

Leaf disease screening

ON-DEVICE MODEL

A compact image classifier runs in this browser. Your image stays on this device.

Select a supported crop and image to begin.

This model screens only bean angular leaf spot, cassava mosaic, and three maize classes (healthy, northern leaf blight, common rust). It cannot identify other crops or diseases. A model score is not diagnostic certainty; field conditions may differ from its training images. Confirm with a local agricultural extension expert before treatment. Model card, classes and limitations.
